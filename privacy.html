Privacy Policy for Brain Quest Trivia

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Apex Mobility System ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Brain Quest Trivia mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

Brain Quest Trivia is a free trivia game. We do not require account creation, registration, or personal login information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to play the game.

However, the app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you for advertising and analytics purposes.

Third-Party Service Providers

The app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data automatically, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) or App Set ID.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from IP addresses for ad geographic targeting.
  • App Activity & Performance: User interactions with ads, video views, crash logs, and diagnostic performance data.

2. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for general audiences (ages 13 and above) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
